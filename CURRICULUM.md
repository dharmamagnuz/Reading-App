# 7-Day Reading Tutor - Phonics Curriculum Specification

## Pedagogical Philosophy: SEE -> HEAR -> REPEAT -> IDENTIFY -> BLEND -> READ

No technical phonetic symbols (like /m/ or IPA) are shown to the child.
Consonant sounds are taught cleanly without adding exaggerated "uh" sounds (e.g. /m/ hum, not "muh").

### Day 1: Letters Have Sounds
- **Goal**: Understand that written letters represent spoken sounds.
- **Consonants**: M, S, T, P
- **Examples**:
  - M -> Mango, Monkey, Moon
  - S -> Sun, Star, Snake
  - T -> Tiger, Tree, Turtle
  - P -> Panda, Pizza, Pen
- **Activities**: Sound Intro, Listen & Choose, Beginning Sound, Sound to Picture Match.

### Day 2: Short Vowel Sounds
- **Goal**: Master the short vowel sounds essential for CVC blending.
- **Vowels**: A (/æ/), E (/e/), I (/ɪ/), O (/ɒ/), U (/ʌ/)
- **Examples**:
  - A -> Apple, Ant, Axe
  - E -> Egg, Elephant, Exit
  - I -> Igloo, Iguana, Ink
  - O -> Octopus, Olive, Ox
  - U -> Umbrella, Up, Under
- **Activities**: Vowel Intro, Vowel Sound Discrimination, Listen and Choose Vowel.

### Day 3: Putting Sounds Together (Two-Letter Blending)
- **Goal**: Visual and auditory blending of two sounds moving together.
- **Combos**:
  - A + M -> AM
  - A + T -> AT
  - I + N -> IN
  - I + T -> IT
  - O + N -> ON
  - U + P -> UP
- **Activities**: Sequential Sound Tap, Animated Letter Merge, Finger-Slide Blending.

### Day 4: First Three-Letter Words (Short A & Short I CVC)
- **Goal**: Decode and blend 3-letter CVC words with short A and I.
- **Words**:
  - cat, bat, mat, sat, pin, sit, big, fin
- **Activities**: Finger-Slide CVC Blending, Picture-to-Word, Word-to-Picture.

### Day 5: More CVC Words (Short E, Short O, Short U)
- **Goal**: Expand CVC decoding across remaining short vowels.
- **Words**:
  - pen, hen, red, dog, pot, log, sun, cup, bus
- **Activities**: Missing Letter (e.g. D _ G -> O), Picture-to-Word, Listen and Find.

### Day 6: First Simple Sentences
- **Goal**: Read short decodable sentences supported by high-frequency helper words.
- **Helper Words**: I, a, is, the, see, on
- **Sentences**:
  - "I see a cat."
  - "A cat is on a mat."
  - "The dog is on the log."
  - "A red hen is on the bus."
  - "I see the big sun."
- **Activities**: Sequential word highlight, word-tap pronunciation, full sentence reading.

### Day 7: Comprehensive Practice & Assessment
- **Goal**: Evaluate child's reading independence across all 7 core phonics domains.
- **Measures**:
  1. Letter-Sound Recognition
  2. Vowel-Sound Recognition
  3. Beginning-Sound Identification
  4. Sound Blending
  5. CVC Word Decoding
  6. Word Recognition
  7. Simple Sentence Reading
- **Scoring Dimensions**:
  - **INDEPENDENT**: Answered without requesting audio hint or retry.
  - **ASSISTED**: Requested audio assistance before answering.
  - **RETRY**: Needed gentle retry after first attempt.
