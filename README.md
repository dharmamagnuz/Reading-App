# Reading Tutor — 7-Day English Reading Tutor for Beginners

A child-friendly, offline-first Android application designed to help beginner young children move from recognizing the English alphabet to reading simple phonetic CVC words and beginner sentences.

## Key Features
- **Structured 7-Day Phonics Curriculum**:
  - Day 1: Letters Have Sounds (Useful Consonants: M, S, T, P)
  - Day 2: Short Vowel Sounds (A, E, I, O, U in beginner CVC context)
  - Day 3: Putting Sounds Together (Two-letter Blending: am, at, in, it, on, up)
  - Day 4: First Three-Letter Words (Short A and Short I CVC Words: cat, mat, sat, pin, sit, etc.)
  - Day 5: More CVC Words (Short E, O, U: pen, hen, red, dog, pot, log, sun, cup, bus)
  - Day 6: First Simple Sentences (Decodable words + helper sight words: I, a, is, the, see, on)
  - Day 7: Comprehensive Practice and Gentle Assessment
- **Pedagogical Phonics Audio Engine**:
  - Clean consonant sounds without distorted "uh" endings (pure /m/, /s/, /t/, /p/).
  - Spoken audio instructions for non-readers.
  - Zero-latency synthesized tone and chime feedback (star pop, gentle retry, success fanfare).
- **Interactive Blending & Activities**:
  - Finger slide blending track with letter-by-letter sound triggers.
  - Sound-to-picture, picture-to-word, word-to-picture, missing letter slots, and listen-and-find.
  - Tappable sentence words with synchronized narration.
- **Supportive, Positive Feedback**:
  - No failure screens or competitive pressure.
  - Gentle "Let's listen again" guidance for retries.
- **Local Progress & Parent Dashboard**:
  - Offline Room database tracking independent, assisted, and retry attempts.
  - Actionable parent reports identifying strong sounds and areas needing practice.
  - Protected child-proof Parent Gate.
