# Audio Recording Master Manifest

This document specifies every spoken audio asset required by the 7-Day Reading Tutor program.

## 1. Phonemes (Pure sounds — without added "uh")
- `phonemes/m.mp3`: Pure /m/ bilabial hum
- `phonemes/s.mp3`: Pure /s/ unvoiced dental fricative
- `phonemes/t.mp3`: Crisp /t/ unvoiced alveolar stop
- `phonemes/p.mp3`: Clean /p/ unvoiced bilabial plosive
- `phonemes/a_short.mp3`: Short /æ/ as in "apple"
- `phonemes/e_short.mp3`: Short /e/ as in "egg"
- `phonemes/i_short.mp3`: Short /ɪ/ as in "igloo"
- `phonemes/o_short.mp3`: Short /ɒ/ as in "octopus"
- `phonemes/u_short.mp3`: Short /ʌ/ as in "umbrella"
- `phonemes/b.mp3`: Crisp /b/ voiced stop
- `phonemes/c.mp3`: Crisp /k/ unvoiced velar stop
- `phonemes/d.mp3`: Crisp /d/ voiced alveolar stop
- `phonemes/f.mp3`: Clean /f/ unvoiced fricative
- `phonemes/g.mp3`: Clean /ɡ/ voiced velar stop
- `phonemes/h.mp3`: Clean /h/ aspirated whisper
- `phonemes/n.mp3`: Clean /n/ nasal sound
- `phonemes/r.mp3`: Clean /r/ liquid sound

- "Tap the letter."
- "Which one starts with this sound?"

- "Let's put the sounds together."
- "Now you try."
- "Listen again."
- "Tap the word you hear."

- "Let's read this sentence together."
